### 🧠 About Me

I'm a **Software Engineer** focused on building scalable, production-grade full-stack applications and exploring the intersection of **AI/ML and product engineering**. My work centers on writing clean, maintainable code, designing systems that scale, and shipping features that solve real user problems — not just demos.

I approach engineering with a **product mindset**: understanding *why* something is being built before writing a single line of code. Comfortable across the stack — from crafting performant, accessible front-ends to designing robust APIs and data models on the backend.

**🎯 Open To:**
- Full-Stack Engineering roles (Frontend / Backend / Full Stack)
- AI/ML Engineering & applied ML roles
- Open-source collaboration
- Freelance / contract product engineering work
